## Production and hands-on development

Ceiba Spaciosa is a 3D project made in Unreal Engine 5. My work combined production with environment tooling, materials, VFX, and player-facing functionality.

## Supporting the environment

Landscaping tools and materials were one part of my contribution; visual effects were another. The environment image gives context for that work, with lighting and drifting particles around the central statue.

## Connecting the player

I also contributed to inventory and player systems. My role crossed the environment workflow and the interactive side of the game, rather than being limited to a single visual effect.
