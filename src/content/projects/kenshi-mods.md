## Expanding an existing world

Martial Village adds a place to discover in Kenshi: a faction to engage with, quest progression, custom dialogue, characters, equipment, and a new starting scenario.

## My contribution

My modding work spans world content, dialogue, assets, and testing under the alias Zheyron. Across the wider catalogue, I have published locations, characters, equipment, gameplay changes, and compatibility patches. Kenshi itself is a game by Lo-Fi Games.

## A community around the work

Martial Village is the most subscribed item in my published catalogue. Its Workshop page connects the release to player discussion, quest questions, bug reports, and compatibility feedback. The walkthrough and bug-report threads give players places to find help and share problems.

My approach to modding joins content creation with follow-through: documenting known issues, communicating workarounds, and making changes informed by testing and reports. The Workshop page remains the reference for current compatibility and change notes.
