## A small movement vocabulary

I designed and prototyped this runner around control of the player’s vertical movement. Space starts a jump from the ground; another press in the air starts a glide. Releasing Space ends the glide, while Control accelerates the descent.

## Readable feedback

Wings appear while gliding, making the active movement mode visible even with simple geometric characters. The sketch also includes a temporary shape change, activated by clicking the player.
