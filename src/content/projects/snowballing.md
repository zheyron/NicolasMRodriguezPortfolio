## The game

Snowballing is a single-player survivor-like built around automatic abilities, a timed run, and upgrades purchased between attempts. A public prototype is available on itch.io.

## My contribution

I developed most of the game from the ground up, taking responsibility for production, game design, and core gameplay programming. I also designed Diovus.

My systems work includes persistent progression, upgrades, health regeneration, interfaces, audio, and gameplay integration.
