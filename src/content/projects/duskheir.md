## Project overview

Duskheir is a Godot project combining my production and design work with hands-on systems development.

## My contribution

I managed the project and revised the initial game design to address constraints in its implementation. I also wrote narrative and worldbuilding material.

My implementation work includes camera and terrain systems, the initial player logic and presentation, player-state changes, and state-aware objects. I created the grass-cutting system, grass particles, and many of the project’s visual effects.

## Public scope

This page summarises my contribution. Internal documents, unpublished worldbuilding, and detailed system specifications are not included.
